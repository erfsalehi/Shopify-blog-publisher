These are not A/B tests, and the design says so. Per-visitor splitting is impossible here — search shows one title to everyone, and Shopify can't show different prices to different visitors. What works, and what SEO practitioners actually do, is a treatment group against a matched control group over the same dates, scored by difference-in-differences. The control group absorbs whatever happened to the whole site — a seasonal dip, an algorithm update — so what's left is attributable to the change.
